How a run works

  1. Collect scrap

    Wrecked robots drop silver scrap. Drive near it and it joins your orbit, up to 12 pieces.

  2. Fire automatically

    Volleys launch at the nearest enemy in range. With an empty orbit, a short magnetic pulse keeps you fighting.

  3. Level up

    Blue energy nuts give XP. Each level offers three upgrades, and solo combat pauses while you choose. Reroll or banish the ones you don’t want.

  4. Hold out

    The swarm grows, bosses join in and elites appear from 4:00. At 15:00 the Scrap Colossus arrives: defeat it to clear the stage. Win or lose, you bank parts for the workshop.

Abilities

Every level-up offers three of the 17 abilities: six weapons, eight support abilities, including the three helper drone roles, and three supplies. Weapons and support abilities rank up for the rest of the run; supplies are one-off boosts you can pick again. The Abilities screen in the main menu describes each one too.

Weapons Up to rank 5

  • Orbiting Saws

    Scrap blades circle your robot and hit nearby enemies. Ranks add damage, orbit size and speed.

  • Chain Lightning

    An arc jumps between 2 enemies for 4 damage every 2.8 seconds. Ranks add targets and damage.

  • Scrap Turret

    Deploys a turret every 8 seconds that fires for 3 damage. Ranks improve fire rate, damage and lifetime.

  • Magnetic Burst

    A shockwave hits nearby enemies for 4 damage and pushes them back every 5 seconds. Ranks widen and strengthen it.

  • Magnet Harpoon

    Every 2.3 seconds a piercing hook flies 8 m the way you’re moving, then reels back, hitting each enemy once each way for 6 damage. Ranks add damage, hooks and faster throws.

  • Slag Mortar

    Every 4.2 seconds a shell lands on the biggest group within 10 m for 3 damage and leaves a burning puddle. Ranks add damage, larger and longer puddles, and a second shell.

Support Up to rank 4

  • Turbo Treads

    Move 12% faster per rank. Slip through gaps and stay ahead of the swarm.

  • Pickup Magnet

    Extends your pickup radius by 0.9 m per rank, so energy and scrap come to you from farther away.

  • Steel Plating

    Cuts contact damage by 2 per rank. Every hit still deals at least 1 damage.

  • Capacitor Bank

    Cuts cooldowns by 8% per rank for every weapon except Orbiting Saws.

  • Field Amplifier

    Widens Magnetic Burst, the Scrap Cyclone and Slag Mortar areas by 10% per rank.

Supplies Repeatable

  • Field Repair

    Restore 35 health right away, up to 100.

  • Scrap Delivery

    Refill all 12 scrap pieces and reset the attack cooldown.

  • Overclock

    +25% damage and +15% speed for 20 seconds. Picking it again refreshes the timer.

Each solo run brings one reroll, which redraws all three choices, and one banish, which removes an upgrade from every later choice. Supplies can’t be banished. Workshop upgrades add more of both.

Helper drone

Every robot starts with a hovering helper drone. Press Q or tap its card to switch roles at any time. Each role has its own upgrade in the level-up choices, up to rank 3, and rank 3 adds a bonus.

  • Collector

    Carries nearby scrap and energy to you. Ranks extend its range and speed up retrieval.

    Rank 3 Pulls up to 5 pickups every 6 seconds.

  • Repair

    Restores 3 health every 5 seconds. Ranks heal more, more often.

    Rank 3 Once per run, heals 20 when you drop to 30 health or less.

  • Guard

    Shoots a nearby enemy every 2 seconds. Ranks add damage and fire rate.

    Rank 3 Shots slow enemies by 50% for 1 second.

Explore the yard

The scrapyard never ends, and the farther you roam, the more you find. Bosses and mini bosses charge across lanes, fire aimed bolts and leave danger zones, so watch for ground warnings before their attacks land. Beating one grants parts, energy, scrap and repairs.

Elite enemies

From 4:00

Some enemies spawn rust-gold, with 3.5× health. Each one pays triple XP, extra scrap and a part. The chance rises from 3% at 4:00 to 10% by 12:00.

Scrap Colossus

Final boss at 15:00

A giant boss that mixes charges, bolt fans and danger zones, and attacks faster below half health. Defeat it to clear the stage, earn 100 parts and win the run.

  • Repair station

    Restores up to 40 health when you need it.

  • Supply chest

    Stay close for 1.2 seconds to open it: 5 XP, 6 scrap and 3 parts.

  • Salvage contract

    Hold its zone for 8 seconds: 12 XP, a full orbit and 8 parts. Progress drains slowly if you leave.

Each discovery pays out once per run.

Stages

Pick where your next run happens from the stage strip on the main menu. Each stage keeps its own best time, and the Daily Shift always runs in the Scrapyard.

The Scrapyard

Always open

The sunlit yard where every robot starts out.

  • Standard enemy health, speed and spawns
  • Elites join in from 4:00
  • The Scrap Colossus arrives at 15:00
  • Defeating it unlocks the Night Shift

Night Shift

Clear the Scrapyard to unlock

The same yard after dark, under cool moonlight with a lamp pool around your robot. A tougher shift that pays better:

  • 40% more enemy health and 20% more spawns
  • Enemies move 10% faster, and more are elites
  • Bosses, including the Scrap Colossus, have 35% more health
  • 50% more parts from every run

Daily Shift

One challenge per calendar day, the same for every player: a fixed seed and one of ten rules, always in the Scrapyard. Start it from Daily Shift on the main menu. A new shift begins at your local midnight.

Daily reward

The first Daily Shift you finish each day pays 20 parts plus 5 per streak day, up to 55 from the seventh day on.

Earning parts

  • Parts from discoveries, elites and bosses, including 100 for the Scrap Colossus
  • 1 part for every 10 kills
  • 1 part for every 30 seconds survived

Parts are banked when a run ends in defeat or a stage clear. Starting over or abandoning a run banks nothing.

Bonuses

  • Night Shift: +50% run parts
  • Salvage License: +10% run parts per rank
  • Work orders and the Daily Shift pay on top

The two run bonuses multiply: a Night Shift run with all three Salvage License ranks pays 1.95× its parts.

Questions

Is Junk Magnet free?

Yes. It’s free to play in your browser, with no account, download or ads.

Does Junk Magnet save my progress?

Parts, unlocked robots, workshop upgrades, stage clears, work orders, Daily Shift streaks and personal bests save in your browser’s local storage. They don’t move between browsers or devices, and clearing browser data removes them. If storage is blocked, progress lasts only for the current session.

Can I play on CrazyGames?

Yes, at crazygames.com/game/junk-magnet. That version keeps your progress in CrazyGames cloud saves, so signing in to CrazyGames syncs it across devices. Progress on CrazyGames and on this site is kept separately.

Can I resume an unfinished run?

Yes, in solo play. The game saves an unfinished run on your device when you pause, switch tabs or close the page, and every 15 seconds. Choose Continue on the main menu to pick it up, even after a reload, including a level-up choice you hadn’t made yet. A game update that changes the save format can discard an old unfinished run.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Drag in the arena to move and tap to choose upgrades. Performance depends on your device and browser; lower the graphics quality in Settings if needed.

Is there multiplayer?

Online co-op is temporarily unavailable while performance issues are fixed. The game currently offers single-player survival.

Which languages and settings are there?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese, chosen in Settings. You can also adjust graphics quality and set music and sound separately, and the game respects your reduced-motion preference.
