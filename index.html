Junk Magnet — Free 3D Browser Survival Game

A tiny robot. An endless scrapyard. Your enemies become your ammunition.

Collect wreckage, build an orbit of scrap, and survive increasingly chaotic robot swarms. Choose from ten abilities, discover three weapon evolutions, and unlock three robots and permanent workshop upgrades.

How to play

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or touch controls. Attacks are automatic: collect silver scrap to reload and blue energy to level up. Choose an upgrade with a click, tap, or keys 1–3.

Play free without an account or download. Workshop progress saves in your browser. The game supports English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.
