## Sound effects — Kenney

- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Sci-fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

The original license notices are included alongside this file as
`kenney-impact-CC0.txt`, `kenney-scifi-CC0.txt`, and `kenney-interface-CC0.txt`.
Selected effects were converted from OGG to mono 32 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV for browser
compatibility. Mixing, positional attenuation and slight pitch variation are applied at runtime.

## Music — Fupi

**Empacotatron**: https://opengameart.org/content/empacotatron
Author: https://opengameart.org/users/fupi
The source page explicitly lists CC0. The author describes an energetic electronic
factory/arcade track and supplies separate looping gameplay and menu arrangements.

- `yard-loop.ogg`: original `empacotatron_loop.ogg` (80 seconds).
- `menu-loop.ogg`: original `empacotatron_menu.ogg`.
- Matching MP3 files: 128 kbps fallback conversions for browsers without OGG decoding.

The OGG originals are unmodified. Music uses low gain and fades between game states.
`public/audio/sources.json` maps every shipped file to its original source filename.
